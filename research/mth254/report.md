18.02 is weak on quadric surfaces, limits/continuity, curvature (11.9) and polar-coordinate review, and it spends time on matrices. These supplements fill the gaps:
- **18.02SC recitations** (CC BY-NC-SA, 5–20 min each): 40 mapped. 20 are core (3.6 h) and 20 optional (3.3 h). Examples: dot products and angles, area using the cross product, equations of planes, lines, vector derivatives, arc length, graphing surfaces, level curves, partials, chain rule, gradient, tangent planes, 2nd derivative test, regions and order of integration, polar, cylindrical volume, spherical.
- **Professor Leonard, Calculus 3** playlist (34 videos, confirmed on the playlist page). 28 are MTH 254-relevant (63.7 h total, 1–3.6 h each). They are long-form drills and good for limits/continuity (13.2), quadric surfaces (11.6), and TNB/curvature (12.3/12.5). All are optional.
- **3Blue1Brown** Essence of Linear Algebra: determinant (10.1 min), dot products and duality (14.2), cross products (8.9), cross products extended (13.2). Optional, for week 1 intuition.
- **Khan Academy multivariable** (Grant Sanderson): the page was blocked by a bot challenge, so `verified:false`.
